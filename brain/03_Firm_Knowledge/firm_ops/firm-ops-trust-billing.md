# Firm ops — trust & billing basics

**Cite:** RPC 1.15 (safekeeping); Office Keeper Ch. 2; jurisdiction-specific IOLTA rules (confirm local)
**Practice areas:** Firm operations | Billing | Ethics

## What this element requires
- Separate operating vs client trust / IOLTA accounts
- Unearned fees / advances in trust until earned; promptly transfer when earned and cleared
- Client ledgers: receipts, disbursements, running balance; monthly three-way reconciliation
- Retain trust records per jurisdictional period (Office Keeper example: 7 years — confirm local rule)
- Written fee agreement; clear flat-fee vs hourly vs hybrid for immigration matters

## Facts that prove it
- Signed fee agreement
- Trust ledger per matter
- Monthly reconciliation reports
- Invoices showing trust applications

## Common pitfalls
- Paying firm expenses directly from trust
- Borrowing between client ledgers
- Flat fees treated as earned before work/ethics rule allows
- Ignoring unclaimed trust funds escheat rules

## Sample language pointers
- Invoice line: “Applied from trust” vs “Due from client”
- Flag negative trust balance as emergency ethics issue
