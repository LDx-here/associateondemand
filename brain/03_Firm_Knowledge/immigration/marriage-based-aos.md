# Marriage-based AOS / immigrant visa

**Cite:** INA §§ 201(b)(2)(A)(i), 204, 216 (conditional residence); 8 CFR § 216; I-130/I-485
**Practice areas:** Family | AOS | Consular | Stokes interview

## What this element requires
- Bona fide marriage at inception (not solely for immigration)
- Petitioner USC or LPR with qualifying status; IR if USC spouse
- Conditional LPR if marriage <2 years at grant — later I-751 joint filing or waiver
- Admissibility / waiver / discretion still required
- Interview readiness: shared life facts, originals, consistent testimony

## Facts that prove it
- Bona fides → joint lease/mortgage, bank accounts, insurance, photos, messages, affidavits, children
- Ceremony / license → civil marriage docs; prior divorce decrees
- Cohabitation timeline → addresses since marriage
- Abuse facts → if I-751 waiver / VAWA path

## Common pitfalls
- Thin bona fides packet for short courtship / large age gap / limited shared language
- Filing without terminating prior marriages
- Travel on advance parole without counseling on risks
- Ignoring conditional residence clock

## Sample language pointers
- Organize exhibits by life domain (finances, residence, social, children)
- Prepare couple for inconsistent secondary details vs core bona fides
