# Withholding of removal & CAT

**Cite:** INA § 241(b)(3); Convention Against Torture; 8 CFR §§ 1208.16–.18
**Practice areas:** Withholding | CAT | Removal defense

## What this element requires
- Available in removal proceedings (not affirmative USCIS asylum office grants)
- **Withholding:** clear probability (>50%) of persecution on a protected ground; mandatory if met and not barred
- **CAT:** more likely than not torture by/with acquiescence of public official; no nexus to protected ground required
- Neither is a path to LPR/citizenship; both can support work authorization
- Bars differ from asylum (e.g., firm resettlement does not bar withholding; PSC thresholds differ)

## Facts that prove it
- Future risk evidence → country conditions, individualized threats
- Torturer identity → government actor or acquiescence facts
- Past torture/persecution as predictive evidence
- Criminal bars screening → AF sentence length for withholding PSC

## Common pitfalls
- Using asylum “well-founded fear” burden language for withholding
- Forgetting CAT when nexus is weak but government torture risk is strong
- Promising green card pathway from withholding/CAT alone

## Sample language pointers
- State burden of proof explicitly for each form of protection
- Separate nexus analysis (withholding) from CAT acquiescence analysis
