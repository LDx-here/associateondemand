# Procedural posture — removal & filing history

**Cite:** INA §§ 239, 240, 241(a)(5); 8 CFR §§ 1003, 1239; EOIR Practice Manual
**Practice areas:** Removal | AOS | Asylum | Posture memos

## What this element requires
- State whether client is: not in proceedings; NTA served but not filed; in IJ proceedings; BIA/appeal; post-order; reinstatement
- Proceedings commence when NTA is **filed with EOIR**, not merely served
- Identify charges: § 212 vs § 237; burden of proof flips accordingly
- Note prior filings: I-485/I-589/I-130, denials, RFEs, motions, voluntary departure, prior orders
- Reinstatement (§ 241(a)(5)) after unlawful reentry post-order sharply limits relief

## Facts that prove it
- NTA / OSC copies; EOIR case number; hearing notices
- Prior orders → IJ/BIA/circuit; expedited removal
- Receipt notices / FOIA / USCIS account history
- Address history for in absentia / notice issues
- ICE encounters / detainers / bond history

## Common pitfalls
- Filing AOS strategies without checking reinstatement / permanent bar
- Missing that USCIS denial may still lead to NTA for priorities / asylum referrals
- Confusing service of NTA with commencement

## Sample language pointers
- Open every strategy memo with a 3–5 line posture block
- List pending forms + next hearing/action date
