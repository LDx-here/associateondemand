# Family-based immigration — IR & preference

**Cite:** INA §§ 201(b), 203(a); Form I-130; Visa Bulletin
**Practice areas:** Family | AOS | Consular

## What this element requires
- Qualifying relationship + petitioner status (USC vs LPR)
- **Immediate relatives (IR):** USC spouse; USC unmarried child <21; USC parent (petitioner ≥21) — no numerical quota
- **Preference:** F1–F4 categories; subject to Visa Bulletin priority dates
- Two-step: I-130 (or I-360) then AOS or consular immigrant visa
- CSPA / surviving relative rules may preserve age or petition — screen when aging out or petitioner dies

## Facts that prove it
- Relationship docs → marriage, birth, adoption, termination of prior marriages
- Petitioner status → naturalization cert, green card, passport
- Priority date → I-130 receipt/approval; chart A vs B filing eligibility
- Bona fides → for marriages, see `marriage-based-aos.md`

## Common pitfalls
- Filing AOS before priority date allows filing (wrong chart)
- Missing prior marriage terminations
- Assuming LPR petitioner can sponsor parent as IR (cannot)

## Sample language pointers
- State category + priority date + current bulletin chart used
- Note derivative beneficiaries and aging-out risk
