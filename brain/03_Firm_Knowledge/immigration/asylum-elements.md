# Asylum elements

**Cite:** INA §§ 101(a)(42), 208; 8 CFR §§ 208, 1208; REAL ID credibility
**Practice areas:** Asylum | Withholding | CAT (related)

## What this element requires
- Refugee definition: past persecution **or** well-founded fear of future persecution
- On account of protected ground: race, religion, nationality, particular social group, political opinion
- Persecutor: government **or** private actor government unwilling/unable to control
- Past persecution → presumption of future fear; DHS may rebut via changed circumstances or reasonable internal relocation
- Credibility under totality (demeanor, consistency, plausibility) — REAL ID
- One-year filing deadline unless changed/extraordinary circumstances + filing within reasonable time thereafter

## Facts that prove it
- Harm narrative → dates, actors, injuries, threats, detentions
- Nexus → persecutor statements, pattern evidence, country conditions
- PSG → immutable/fundamental trait, particularity, social distinction (circuit-sensitive)
- Corroboration → medical, police reports, declarations, country reports
- Timeline → U.S. arrival date; reasons for delayed filing

## Common pitfalls
- Conclusory PSG without particularity/social distinction
- Ignoring one-year bar / exception proof
- Inconsistencies across I-589, credible fear, and testimony
- Treating withholding/CAT standards as identical to asylum

## Sample language pointers
- Structure: credibility → past harm → nexus → future fear → relocation → discretion
- Cite country conditions exhibits by name/date
