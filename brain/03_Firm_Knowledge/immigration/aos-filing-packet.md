# AOS filing packet & process checklist

**Cite:** Form I-485 instructions; INA § 245; USCIS filing guidance
**Practice areas:** AOS | Family | Employment

## What this element requires
- Current form editions + correct filing location/fees
- Core: I-485; civil surgeon I-693 (timing strategy — validity rules change; confirm current PM); identity/status docs; translations
- Family-based: I-130 (concurrent if eligible) or approval + I-864 package
- § 245(i): I-485 Supp. A + penalty fee + grandfathering proof
- Optional concurrent I-765 / I-131 (EAD/AP) when eligible
- Biometrics → interview → RFE practice; update address within 10 days

## Facts that prove it
- Checklist of forms filed + receipt numbers
- Medical exam date / civil surgeon
- Financial packet date (may need refresh at interview)
- Interview notices / RFE deadlines

## Common pitfalls
- Expired medicals due to long pendency
- Missing certified translations
- Abandonment from missed RFE/interview after address change
- Concurrent filing when priority date not current

## Sample language pointers
- Maintain a living exhibit/form checklist in the matter
- Note what must be updated at interview
